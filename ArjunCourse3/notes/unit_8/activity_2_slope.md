# VIII-2 Slope

[KEY]
**Slope** `m` measures steepness. `m = Δy/Δx = rise/run = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁)`. The **further from 0**, the **steeper** (use absolute value; `−8` is steeper than `5`). Positive = up right; negative = down right; **0** = horizontal; **undefined** = vertical (`Δx = 0`, do not divide by 0). Same-line slope triangles are **similar**.
[/KEY]

## Quick Review Notes

Class packet **VIII-2 Slope/Rate of Change/Linear Equations: Slope** (Course 3, Classroom 8-13). After graphing in VIII-1, measure how steep the line is.

### Formula
`m = Δy / Δx = (change in y) / (change in x) = (vertical change) / (horizontal change) = rise / run = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)`

### Types of slope
| Type | Picture | `m` |
|------|---------|-----|
| Positive | Up to the right | `m > 0` |
| Negative | Down to the right | `m < 0` |
| Slope of 0 | Horizontal | `m = 0` (rise 0) |
| Undefined | Vertical | no `m` (`run = 0`) |

### Guided examples (VIII-2 notes)

**From a graph (1–3)**

| # | Points on the line | Slope | Type |
|---|--------------------|-------|------|
| 1 | `(−4, −1)` and `(2, 1)` | `m = (1 − (−1)) / (2 − (−4)) = 2/6 = 1/3` | Positive |
| 2 | `(−2, 3)` and `(3, 2)` | `m = (2 − 3) / (3 − (−2)) = −1/5` | Negative |
| 3 | `(−1, 5)` and `(6, 5)` | `m = 0/7 = 0` | Zero (horizontal) |

**From two points (4–6)**

| # | Points | Work | `m` |
|---|--------|------|-----|
| 4 | `(1, −2)` and `(7, −2)` | `(−2 − (−2)) / (7 − 1) = 0/6` | **0** |
| 5 | `(2, −4)` and `(3, 4)` | `(4 − (−4)) / (3 − 2) = 8/1` | **8** |
| 6 | `(1, 8)` and `(1, 3)` | `(3 − 8) / (1 − 1) = −5/0` | **Undefined** |

**From a table (7)**  
`x`: 1, 4, 7, 10  `y`: 8, 6, 4, 2  
Use `(1, 8)` and `(4, 6)`: `m = (6 − 8) / (4 − 1) = −2/3`. Any other pair on the table gives the same slope.

**Which is steeper? (8–9)**  
The further `|m|` is from 0, the steeper.

| # | Slopes | Steeper |
|---|--------|---------|
| 8 | A: `7/3` ≈ 2.33, B: `2` | **A** (`7/3`) |
| 9 | A: `5`, B: `−8` | **B** (`−8`, since 8 > 5) |

**Slope triangles (10–13)**  
Line through `A(3, 2)`, `B(6, 4)`, `D(9, 6)`, `E(15, 10)`. Drop a right angle at `C(6, 2)` and at `F(15, 6)`.

| Segment | Length |
|---------|--------|
| `AC` | **3** (horizontal 3 to 6) |
| `BC` | **2** (vertical 2 to 4) |
| `DF` | **6** (horizontal 9 to 15) |
| `EF` | **4** (vertical 6 to 10) |

`BC/AC = 2/3` and `EF/DF = 4/6 = 2/3` (same slope). **Triangle ABC and triangle DEF are similar.**

## You Try slips
- Subtracting a negative: `4 − (−4) = 8`, not 0.
- Vertical is **undefined**, not “no slope written as 0.”
- Steeper uses **distance from 0**, not “the positive one” and not “the bigger looking fraction without comparing.”
