# VII-5 Solve for y

[KEY]
**Solve for y** means rearrange so `y` is alone: `y = mx + b` (slope-intercept). Move the `x` terms and constants off y’s side, then divide by y’s coefficient. Watch the sign when that coefficient is negative. Fractions: multiply through to clear, then isolate. Decimals: ×10 first, same as VII-3.
[/KEY]

## Quick Review Notes

Class packet **VII-5 / 10-? Solve For Y** (Course 3). After solving for `x` in VII-1–VII-4, isolate **y** so the equation is ready to graph.

### Class method

1. Expand parentheses and combine like terms.
2. Get all **y** terms on one side.
3. Move **x** terms and constants to the other side.
4. Divide (or multiply) so `y` has coefficient 1.
5. Write `y = mx + b` with simplified fractions.

## Common slips
- Forgetting to flip the sign when dividing by a **negative** y-coefficient (`−2y` or `−9y`).
- Leaving `y` with a coefficient (`4y = −5x + 20` instead of `y = −(5/4)x + 5`).
- Distributing only the `x` in `7(x − 11)` or `10(y + 1)`.
- On decimals (#12), clearing with ×10 instead of matching both `6.5` and `1.3`.
