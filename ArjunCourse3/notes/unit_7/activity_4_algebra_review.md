# VII-R Solve Equations Algebraically: Review

[KEY]
Solve: simplify each side, move variable terms, move constants, divide. Variables cancel with a **false** leftover → **no solution**. Variables cancel with a **true** leftover → **infinite**. Decimals: multiply through to clear tenths. Two pay plans that meet: set member cost = nonmember cost. Two scores that add to `T`: write both in terms of `T` and set the sum equal to `T`.

### Word problems (9–11)

**9. Camping.** Member: `$45 + $35` per night. Nonmember: `$40` per night.
`45 + 35n = 40n` → **`n = 9` nights**.

**10–11. 2020 NFC (VII-R).** 49ers: 1 less than `2/3` of total `T`. Packers: 56 less than `4/3` of `T`. Together they scored all `T` points.
`(2/3)T − 1 + (4/3)T − 56 = T` → `2T − 57 = T` → **`T = 57`**.
49ers `37`, Packers `20` — **49ers won by 17**.
