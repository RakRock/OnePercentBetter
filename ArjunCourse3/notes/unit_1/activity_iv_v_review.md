# Concept IV + V Review WS

[KEY]
**IV:** same base → add/subtract/multiply exponents. Negative exponent = reciprocal. Anything nonzero to the 0 power is **1**. Power of a product: raise **every** factor. Solve by matching exponents after rewriting with one base.
**V:** scientific notation is `a × 10ⁿ` with **1 ≤ |a| < 10**. Convert; then multiply/divide coefficients and add/subtract exponents; add/subtract only after matching the power of 10. Rewrite if `|a|` is not in `[1, 10)`.
[/KEY]

## Quick Review Notes

Class packet **Concept IV + V Review WS** (before CC 9). Mix of exponent properties and scientific notation. Also review **CCs 5–7**.

### IV — exponential form, positive exponents only

| # | Problem | Answer |
|---|---------|--------|
| 1 | `x²⁵ · x⁷` | **`x³²`** |
| 2 | `5⁸ / 5¹²` | **`1/5⁴`** |
| 3 | `8675309⁰` | **`1`** |
| 4 | `1 / 3⁻⁴` | **`81`** (`3⁴`) |
| 5 | `(x⁶)²` | **`x¹²`** |
| 6 | `(2 · 7⁵) / (2⁸ · 7⁻¹¹)` | **`7¹⁶ / 2⁷`** |
| 7 | `5⁻⁴ / 10⁹` | **`1 / (5⁴ · 10⁹)`** |
| 8 | `(8x⁴ y)³` | **`512 x¹² y³`** |
| 9 | `(8x⁴ y⁸) / (24 x⁻³ y⁹)` | **`x⁷ / (3y)`** |
| 10 | `(5x³ y⁻⁷)(4x⁹ y¹²)` | **`20 x¹² y⁵`** |
| 11 | Quadruple `4⁹` | **`4¹⁰`** (`4 · 4⁹ = 4¹⁺⁹`) |
| 12 | `3ˣ · 3⁹ = 3⁹⁰⁰` | **`x = 891`** |
| 13 | `(10ˣ)⁴ = 10³⁰` | **`x = 15/2`** |

**#4:** `1 / 3⁻⁴ = 3⁴ = 81` — a negative exponent in the **denominator** flips to a positive power.
**#6:** split the factors: `2¹⁻⁸ · 7⁵⁻(⁻¹¹) = 2⁻⁷ · 7¹⁶ = 7¹⁶ / 2⁷`.
**#12:** add exponents first: `3ˣ⁺⁹ = 3⁹⁰⁰`, so `x + 9 = 900`. Do not treat this like `(3ˣ)⁹`.
**#13:** `(10ˣ)⁴ = 10⁴ˣ`, so `4x = 30` and `x = 15/2`. Subtracting `30 − 4` is the CC6 #8 slip.

### V — scientific notation

| # | Problem | Answer |
|---|---------|--------|
| 14 | `0.000831` | **`8.31 × 10⁻⁴`** |
| 15 | `90,900` | **`9.09 × 10⁴`** |
| 16 | `1.83 × 10⁴` | **`18,300`** |
| 17 | `9.24 × 10⁻⁵` | **`0.0000924`** |
| 18 | `(1.3 × 10⁴)(2 × 10⁻⁸)` | **`2.6 × 10⁻⁴`** |
| 19 | `2.3 × 10⁸ + 4.3 × 10¹⁰` | **`4.323 × 10¹⁰`** |
| 20 | `(3 × 10¹⁰⁰⁰) − (1.7 × 10⁹⁹⁹)` | **`2.83 × 10¹⁰⁰⁰`** |
| 21 | `(1.3 × 10⁻⁶) + 0.00019` | **`1.913 × 10⁻⁴`** |
| 22 | `(1.2 × 10⁻⁸) / (3 × 10¹²)` | **`4 × 10⁻²¹`** |

**Add/subtract:** rewrite to the **same** power of 10, then combine coefficients, then fix scientific form.
**#20:** `1.7 × 10⁹⁹⁹ = 0.17 × 10¹⁰⁰⁰`, then `3 − 0.17 = 2.83`.
**#21:** `0.00019 = 1.9 × 10⁻⁴` and `1.3 × 10⁻⁶ = 0.013 × 10⁻⁴`.
**#22:** `1.2/3 = 0.4` and `−8 − 12 = −20`, so `0.4 × 10⁻²⁰ = 4 × 10⁻²¹`.

## Common slips
- `#4`: leaving `1/3⁻⁴` or writing `1/81`.
- `#12`: subtracting `900 − 9` or multiplying `x · 9`.
- `#13`: `x = 30 − 4 = 26`.
- `#19–21`: adding coefficients without matching the power of 10.
- `#22`: stopping at `0.4 × 10⁻²⁰` instead of `4 × 10⁻²¹`.
