# 🦁 Chapter 3: Mali and the Lion King

**About 1235–1600 CE**

After Ghana grew weak, the **Mali Empire** rose in its place. Its story begins with a legend that people passed down by speaking it aloud.

## Sundiata, the Lion King

According to oral history, Prince **Sundiata Keita** was born weak and could not walk. A cruel king conquered his home and killed his brothers. Sundiata had special iron leg braces made, learned to walk, gathered an army, and won the **Battle of Kirina** in **1235 CE**. That victory began Mali.

## Mansa Musa's pilgrimage

The most famous king of Mali was **Mansa Musa**. **Mansa** means emperor. He is remembered as one of the wealthiest people who ever lived. In **1324 CE**, as a devout Muslim, he made the **Hajj**, the pilgrimage to Mecca.

He traveled with about **60,000** people and camels loaded with gold. In **Cairo**, Egypt, he gave away so much gold that gold became common and its value crashed. That inflation took more than 10 years to settle.

## Timbuktu, a city of books

Mansa Musa used his wealth to build **Timbuktu** into a world center of learning. At the **University of Sankore**, scholars studied medicine, astronomy, and law.
