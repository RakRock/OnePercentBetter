# 🛶 Chapter 4: Songhai and the War Canoes

**About 1464–1591 CE**

As Mali grew weaker, **Songhai** broke away and conquered its neighbors. It became the largest of these West African empires.

## Sunni Ali's navy

**Sunni Ali**, the founder, was a general who fought on horseback for much of his life. He also built a navy of armored **war canoes**. They sailed the Niger River and captured cities quickly.

## Askia the Great

The next major leader, **Askia Muhammad**, saw that the empire was too big for one person to run alone. He divided it into provinces with governors. He also made every marketplace use the same weights and scales, so sellers could not cheat buyers.

## Defeat at Tondibi

Songhai looked unbeatable until **1591**, when an army from **Morocco** crossed the desert. Songhai warriors had spears and bows. Morocco brought **muskets and cannons**. Songhai lost the **Battle of Tondibi**, and the age of these great West African empires came to an end.
