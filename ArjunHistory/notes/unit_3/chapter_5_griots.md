# 🪕 Chapter 5: Griots, the Living Libraries

**Culture**

Imagine a country with no computers, no books, and no paper for writing down its history. How would anyone remember what happened? West African kingdoms used a **griot**.

## The living library

A griot (say it **GREE-oh**) is a traditional storyteller, historian, and musician. Griots memorized hundreds of years of family lines, battles, and laws.

## A job you inherit

You did not apply to become a griot. The title passed from parent to child. Griots practiced for decades. They sang long historical stories while playing the **kora**, an instrument with 21 strings, often built from a large gourd.

## Kente cloth and masks

Artists wove bright, geometric **kente** cloth in strips. Only kings were allowed to wear it. Artists also carved wooden **masks** used in dances that honored the spirits of ancestors.
