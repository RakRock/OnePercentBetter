# 💡 Quick check

Answer these out loud, then check the answer.

1. **What did West African empires do to get rich if they did not dig up the salt or gold themselves?**  
   They sat in the middle of the trade routes, like a toll booth, and taxed the traders who passed through.

2. **Why did Mansa Musa travel all the way across Africa?**  
   He was a Muslim making the Hajj, the pilgrimage to Mecca, in 1324 CE.

3. **What is a griot?**  
   A traditional storyteller and musician who keeps a kingdom's history in memory. A living library.
