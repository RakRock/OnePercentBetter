# 💡 Quick review

Answer these out loud, then check the answer.

1. **Why were camels so important to the Bedouins?**  
   They could survive long trips in the harsh desert with very little water. People called them the ships of the desert.

2. **What is the Hijrah?**  
   Muhammad's move from Mecca to Medina in 622 CE. The Islamic calendar starts from this year.

3. **If you are doing math homework with equations, which Golden Age discovery are you using?**  
   Algebra, from al-Khwarizmi. The numerals 0–9, including zero, also came into Europe through Muslim scholars.
