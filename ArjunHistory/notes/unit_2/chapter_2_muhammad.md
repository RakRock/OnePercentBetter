# 📜 Chapter 2: The Honest Merchant

**The Prophet Muhammad**

In the busy trading town of Mecca, there was a man named **Muhammad**, born in **570 CE**. He worked as a merchant and was so trustworthy that people nicknamed him "The Trustworthy One." His first wife, **Khadijah**, was a wealthy and respected businesswoman. She was the first person to accept his teachings.

## The cave experience

Muhammad used to go to a quiet cave on **Mount Hira**, outside Mecca, to think and meditate. In **610 CE**, Muslims believe the angel **Gabriel** visited him there and gave him messages from God. In Arabic, God is called **Allah**.

Those messages were later written in the **Quran**, which Muslims consider the word of God. The recorded habits, actions, and sayings of Muhammad are called the **Sunnah**.

## The core message

Muhammad taught that there is only one God to worship. That belief is called **monotheism**. He also taught that rich people should share their wealth with the poor.

At the time, Mecca worshipped many different statues and gods. This new message was a massive shift. Wealthy merchants feared they would lose trade and the pilgrims who came for the old shrines.

## The great road trip: the Hijrah

The leaders of Mecca did not like Muhammad's new ideas because they threatened their power. In **622 CE**, Muhammad and his followers packed up and moved to a city called **Medina** (Yathrib). Medina means "City of the Prophet."

This migration is called the **Hijrah**. It was such a big turning point that the Islamic calendar starts counting its years from this event. It marks the first independent Muslim community, the **Ummah**.

In **630 CE**, Muhammad and his followers returned to Mecca and removed the statues from the Kaaba, dedicating it to the worship of one God. Muhammad died in Medina in **632 CE**.
