# 🖐️ Chapter 3: The 5 Rules of the Game

**The Five Pillars of Islam**

Just like every good game or sport has rules, Islam has five core practices that Muslims follow. These are the **Five Pillars of Islam**.

1. **The Declaration (Shahada).** Openly stating, "There is no god but God, and Muhammad is His messenger."
2. **The Daily Prayers (Salat).** Pausing to pray five times a day, always facing the Kaaba in Mecca. Before prayer, Muslims wash in a ritual called **wudu**. A **mosque** is a Muslim house of worship. The call to prayer is the **adhan**, chanted from a tower called a **minaret**. An **imam** leads the prayers. On Friday, the community gathers for congregational prayer, which includes a sermon called the **khutbah**.
3. **The Giving Tree (Zakat).** Giving a portion of your wealth — traditionally **2.5%** of surplus savings — to help the poor, sick, and needy.
4. **The Daylight Fast (Sawm).** During the holy month of **Ramadan**, not eating or drinking from sunrise until sunset. It teaches self-control and helps people understand what it feels like to be hungry. The festival at the end of Ramadan is **Eid al-Fitr**.
5. **The Ultimate Road Trip (Hajj).** Traveling at least once in your life, if you are able, to Mecca to pray with people from around the world. Pilgrims wear simple white clothing called **ihram** to show that everyone is equal. The festival at the end of the Hajj is **Eid al-Adha**, the Festival of Sacrifice.
