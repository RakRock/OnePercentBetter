# 💡 Quick check

Answer these out loud, then check the answer.

1. **How did someone earn a government job in Tang and Song China?**  
   By passing a hard exam on Confucianism. Family wealth was not enough. That is a meritocracy.

2. **Who held the real power in feudal Japan?**  
   The shogun, the military ruler. The emperor was mostly a religious figurehead.

3. **What is Bushido?**  
   The samurai honor code: loyalty, bravery, and self-discipline. It means "the way of the warrior."
