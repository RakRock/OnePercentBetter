# ⚔️ Chapter 2: The Land of the Samurai

**Feudal Japan**

Imagine an island country where the person wearing the crown has almost no real power, and a supreme military general runs the kingdom. That was feudal Japan.

## The power pyramid

Japan's government was a steep triangle.

1. **The emperor** sat at the top, but was mostly a religious figurehead.
2. **The shogun** was the military ruler who held the real power and made the laws.
3. **The daimyo** were wealthy warlords who owned huge private estates.
4. **The samurai** were elite, heavily armored warriors who swore loyalty to a daimyo.
5. **Peasants and merchants** grew the food and carried on trade at the bottom.

## The code of Bushido

Samurai lived by a strict honor code called **Bushido**, "the way of the warrior." It demanded loyalty to their lord, bravery, and self-discipline. If a samurai lost that honor, he was expected to perform ritual suicide to restore the family name.

## Zen Buddhism and Shinto

Japanese culture mixed two paths.

- **Shinto** is the ancient religion of Japan. It focuses on respecting nature spirits called **kami**.
- **Zen Buddhism** traveled from China. It taught deep focus, meditation, and mental control. Samurai liked it because they needed to stay calm in battle.
