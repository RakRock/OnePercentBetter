# 🏹 Chapter 3: The Holy Wars

**The Crusades**

Think of a tug-of-war, fought for generations, over one sacred city: **Jerusalem**.

## The call to arms

In **1095 CE**, **Pope Urban II** told European knights to march to the Middle East and take Jerusalem back from Muslim control. He promised that anyone who fought would have their sins forgiven.

## The clash

For nearly 200 years, waves of Christian crusaders fought Muslim armies. One of the Muslim generals was **Saladin**.

## The accidental upside

The crusaders did not hold Jerusalem. The wars still changed Europe. Knights came home wanting Eastern goods: silk, spices, lemons, and sugar. They also brought back Greek and Roman medical, mathematical, and scientific knowledge that Muslim scholars had preserved. Trade between Europe and the Middle East boomed.
