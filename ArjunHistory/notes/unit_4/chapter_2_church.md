# ⛪ Chapter 2: The Power of the Catholic Church

Medieval Europe had many countries, languages, and kings. One institution tied people together: the **Roman Catholic Church**.

## The pope's power

The **pope**, the head of the Church, was often more powerful than any European king. If a king disobeyed, the pope could order **excommunication**: banish the king from the Church and tell his people they no longer had to obey him.

## The ticket to heaven

Life was brutal and short. The Church taught that the way into heaven was through the **sacraments**, holy rituals such as baptism and communion. Local priests controlled those rituals, so the Church had spiritual power over everyone.

## Monks and monasteries

While most people farmed, **monks** lived apart in **monasteries**. They hand-copied ancient books, and their monasteries ran the first basic hospitals and schools. They were Europe's librarians.
