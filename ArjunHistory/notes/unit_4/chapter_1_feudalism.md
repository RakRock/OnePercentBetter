# 🛡️ Chapter 1: The Contract System

**The feudal pyramid**

After the Roman Empire collapsed, Europe became dangerous and full of bandits. To stay safe, people made a deal: "You give me protection, and I will give you my work and loyalty." That system is called **feudalism**.

## The loyalty pyramid

- **The king** owned all the land but could not protect it alone. He divided it into pieces called **fiefs** and gave them to wealthy lords.
- **Lords**, also called **vassals**, ran the large estates. They promised soldiers to the king whenever a war broke out.
- **Knights** were heavily armored warriors on horseback. They followed **chivalry**: bravery, politeness, and protecting the weak.
- **Serfs** were peasants tied to the land. They were not slaves, and they were not free. They farmed all day in exchange for a safe place to sleep inside the castle walls.

## The manor economy

Every estate was a **manor**, a self-sufficient miniature town. It had its own mill, blacksmith, church, and fields. People rarely traveled more than a few miles from where they were born, because everything they needed was right there.
