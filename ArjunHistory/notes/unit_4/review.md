# 💡 Quick check

Answer these out loud, then check the answer.

1. **What is a serf?**  
   A peasant farmer legally tied to a lord's manor. Not a slave, and not free.

2. **What was the main unintended result of the Crusades?**  
   Trade grew between Europe and the Middle East. Knights brought home new goods and ideas, including knowledge Muslim scholars had saved.

3. **Why is the Magna Carta such a big deal?**  
   It said even a king must follow the law, and it introduced trial by jury.
